# Hopster — Market Research & Competitive Analysis

> Snapshot compiled to inform Hopster's positioning. All comparables
> studied are public-data observations from app-intelligence dashboards
> (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, GameRefinery, data.ai) — readers should
> independently verify current figures before committing budget.

## 1. Why this genre, why now

### Endless arcade is *the* lowest-floor category
- Top-N lifetime downloads on the App Store almost always includes
  Subway Surfers, Temple Run, Crossy Road. Endless runners and
  endless hoppers have **never** left the top charts since 2011.
- Sessions are short enough for commute / queue / waiting, which
  matches mobile's #1 use case.

### Modern trends a 2026 launch must absorb
1. **Vertical-first design** — TikTok, Reels, and Shorts trained users
   to swipe **up**. Vertical orientation reads natively.
2. **Skin economies > level packs** — Fortnite, Brawl Stars, Stumble
   Guys: revenue ≫ from cosmetics than from content.
3. **Battle Pass is the new sub** — Apex, Fortnite, even Subway
   Surfers added a season pass. Players prefer flat-fee progression
   over per-item purchases.
4. **Hyper-casual is dying as a pure genre**; **"hybrid casual"** is
   replacing it (Voodoo's pivot in 2023). Lean toward deeper meta.
5. **Short-form viral compatibility** — your game must produce
   shareable 15-second moments organically.
6. **Live-ops is mandatory** — week-1 retention is decided by
   "is there something new since I last played?"
7. **Accessibility scrutiny** — Apple/Google have been highlighting
   accessibility-friendly titles in editorial slots.

## 2. Competitive landscape

### 2.1 Crossy Road · Hipster Whale
- **What works**: Voxel charm, character roster, "step on tap" — a
  one-finger control scheme. Free with rewarded ads only at launch.
- **What's dated**: No real meta-progression beyond character unlocks.
  No seasonal content beyond holidays. Music is the *same* loop.
- **What we steal**: Endless lane format. Character collection.
- **What we beat**: Combo / score multiplier. Biomes. Hop Pass.

### 2.2 Subway Surfers · SYBO + Kiloo
- **What works**: A new biome every 2 weeks for 10+ years. THE
  textbook live-ops case study. Daily reward loop is best in class.
  Vertical-first.
- **What's dated**: Forced auto-run reduces strategy. Some predatory
  mystery boxes.
- **What we steal**: Live-ops cadence. Daily reward streak. Skin
  curation calendar.
- **What we beat**: Grid-based puzzle layer (more brain than reflex).
  Less invasive monetization.

### 2.3 Crossy Road Castle · Hipster Whale
- **What works**: Co-op coop play, vertical scrolling tower.
- **What's hard**: Apple Arcade exclusive — no F2P signal.
- **What we steal**: Tower-tile generator architecture.
- **What we beat**: F2P model that actually exists.

### 2.4 Stumble Guys · Scopely
- **What works**: Mass appeal, party game, $250M+ in 2023.
- **What's relevant**: Skin-driven economy with no PvE pay-to-win.
  Validates skin-only IAP at scale.
- **Lessons**: Lean hard into cosmetics; don't add weapon/stat IAP.

### 2.5 Vampire Survivors · poncle
- **Lesson**: Auto-attack genres with *unlock progression* between
  runs are the new dominant casual format. Hopster's run-feeds-meta
  loop matches this pattern.

### 2.6 Geometry Dash · RobTop
- **Lesson**: A single-mechanic skill game with massive replay value
  thrives via **community-generated levels**. Future Hopster v2.0
  could add a level editor for player-made gauntlets.

## 4. Player psychology — what makes a hopper hook

1. **Just-one-more-run loop**: short fail state + instant retry. Our
   game-over to restart is 1 tap.
2. **Visible progress per run**: combo number, score, gridY,
   collectibles. Always show *something* climbing.
3. **Tease the next reward**: "3 hops until next chest", "next skin in
   500 coins". Casino-style salience without dark patterns.
4. **Sharable moments**: dramatic almost-deaths, near-perfect combos.
   Auto-record + share button in v0.5.
5. **Variable reward schedules**: drop tables, daily quests, random
   biome shifts. Backed by 80 years of behavioural psych research
   (Skinner et al.) — but ours respect the player.

## 5. Why current Frogger games fail

- *Frogger in Toy Town* (Apple Arcade) — gorgeous but locked
  behind subscription; no daily hook.
- *Frogger Beyond / Forever* — overcomplicated; lost the 1981 verb.
- Multiple shovelware clones — no live-ops, no retention loops, dead.

Konami owns *Frogger* the IP — we cannot use the name. **"Hopster"**
is a strong working title; legal sweep before launch.

## 6. Cultural & geographic positioning

- **US/EU/JP**: lean cosmetic, ironic skin tones, Battle Pass.
- **SE Asia / India**: lean toward larger free-track rewards; meet
  lower price ceilings ($0.99 SKUs); shorter session preference.
- **MENA**: localize event biomes for cultural fit (e.g., desert and
  mosaic-tile look for an Eid event).
- **China (deferred)**: requires a local publisher and ISBN approval;
  not in v1.0 scope.

## 7. Differentiation summary

| Pillar | Hopster's edge |
|---|---|
| **Verb** | Frogger discipline > Crossy Road's pure timing |
| **Camera** | Auto-scrolling pressure → tension every second |
| **Meta** | Hop Pass + skins-with-perks (sidegrades only) |
| **Live-ops** | Weekly biome event, monthly drop, seasonal pass |
| **Viral** | Combo flex + biome bragging are TikTok-native moments |
| **Ethics** | Drop rates shown, no FOMO timers, refund FAQ visible |

## 8. Risk reading (market-side)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Hyper-casual death | Hybrid-casual depth; meta-prog from week 1 |
| Big-pub clone in 90 days | Move fast in P1–P3, defend with live-ops cadence |
| iOS privacy hits UA | Lean on rewarded video + Hop Pass to lift LTV |
| TikTok saturation | Diversify creator marketing across YouTube Shorts + Reels |
| Lawsuits over "Frogger" similarity | Different name, different ruleset, never reference original |

## 9. What we should validate in soft launch

These hypotheses become our soft-launch experiments:

1. **H1**: Combo system increases session length by ≥ 30% vs. a
   no-combo build (A/B remove combo).
2. **H2**: Skins with perks convert at ≥ 1.5× rate of cosmetic-only
   skins (A/B price-equalized).
3. **H3**: Daily quests lift D7 retention by ≥ 4pp (A/B disable quests).
4. **H4**: Hop Pass at $4.99 outperforms $2.99 on revenue per impression
   despite lower conversion.
5. **H5**: Train lane is "fun-scary" (negative correlation with rage-
   quits), not "frustrating" (positive correlation).

We ship the live game ready to **kill** any feature that doesn't earn
its keep on these metrics.
